authenticated actor in a complete authorization predicate can be documented

commitment, or generic messaging signatures are not implicitly adopted as
the management command's transport. The two known omissions are documentary
FAIL, not observed attacks or a fourteenth runtime failure. Their repairs

## Evidence replay and execution

This is **reviewed-document replay**, not automatic semantic analysis of
arbitrary prose. The independent review catalog supplies reasoning and full
cited ranges. The checker requires all sixteen committed Git blobs to match
length/SHA-256, every cited range, and independently frozen W3C publication
and statement identity. Different, missing, extra, malformed or changed
bytes yield ERROR, not a normative FAIL. Only this exact reviewed revision
can reproduce the 49 author judgments and seven completion assessments.

Run with output files outside the subject checkout:

```sh
python3 scripts/inspect_method_document.py \
  --repository /path/to/sage-spec \
  --out /path/to/reports/method-document.json \
  --raw-out /path/to/reports/method-document-raw.json \
  --inspector-revision INSPECTOR_COMMIT
```

Committed objects are read with replacement refs disabled; working files,
subject code, branches and hooks do not supply prose or verdicts. No DID is
registered and no transaction is made. The separate `method-document-reports`
CI artifact contains raw bytes, report, reviewed catalog and trace coverage;
it is never combined with `sage-projection-reports`.
